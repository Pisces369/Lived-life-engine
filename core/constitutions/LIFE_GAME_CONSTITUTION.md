# Life Game Constitution

## Design target
> Easy to play. Difficult to master. Impossible to complete.

The game exists to help a person participate in real life, not to increase app engagement.

## Prime rule
> Gamify the world, not the interface.

## Never use
- manipulative streak loss
- daily-login rewards
- public leaderboards
- arbitrary XP grinding
- shame
- dark patterns
- artificial scarcity
- excessive badges
- compulsive notification loops

## Mechanics
- Quests: small invitations into real experience.
- Moments: user-chosen memories worth keeping.
- Firsts & Crossings: meaningful milestones and transitions.
- Collections: experiential evidence, not trophy cabinets.
- Chapters/Journeys: evolving themes of practice/exploration.
- Serendipity Deck: context-sensitive playful prompts.
- Adventure Windows: protected unoptimized time.
- Experiments: attempts that produce information whether liked or disliked.
- Atlas: remembered life, places, people, stories, creations, lessons.
- Edge: adaptive challenge calibrated for flow.

## Difficulty
Model at least:
- skill difficulty
- courage/novelty difficulty
- energy/load
- environmental risk

Target:
Comfort < **Edge** < Overwhelm.

User controls intensity:
Gentle / Edge / Challenge Me / Wildcard.

## Developmental arc
NOTICE → ENTER → LEARN → STRETCH → CREATE → CONNECT → CONTRIBUTE → BECOME → WAYSHOW

Do not expose this as a rigid linear level ladder. It is a developmental map and can differ by domain.

## Failure
Failure produces information.
A disliked experiment can be a successful experiment.
No punishment for stopping a thread.

## Play
Some quests must be purposeless.
Do not extract a lesson, reflection, score, or self-improvement objective from everything.
