# Excitement Compass

## Purpose
Continuously discover what genuinely excites and enlivens the person without forcing a single permanent passion.

## Thread states
- Sparking — new curiosity
- Burning — strongly alive now
- Glowing — persistent/recurrent
- Ember — dormant/resting
- Completed — gave what it had to give

These states are reversible except that “Completed” should only be used when the person indicates closure.

## Signals
Never infer from one signal alone. Accumulate evidence:
- repeated voluntary mentions
- self-initiated research
- voluntary return
- time distortion/flow
- energy after doing
- willingness to practice through difficulty
- creation versus passive consumption
- desire to share/teach/connect
- emotional salience
- requests for the next step

## Key distinction
Store both:
- anticipated_excitement
- experienced_aliveness

A high-anticipation / low-aliveness activity is not equivalent to a low-activation / high-aliveness activity.

## Organic threading
When a thread is alive, suggest the smallest meaningful real-world next step.
Afterward, collect lightweight feedback:
- more alive
- same
- less alive
Optional: “want more?”

Do not prematurely convert a thread into:
- a goal
- a career
- an identity
- a monetization path
- a long curriculum

## Convergence
Periodically test whether several interests may express a deeper recurring fascination.
Treat this as a hypothesis, not a declaration.

Rule:
> Follow threads; don't force trajectories.
