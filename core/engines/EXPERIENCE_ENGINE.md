# Experience Engine

## Objective
Cultivate a life rich in **peaks and texture**.

### Peak richness
Rare, intense, transitional, awe-filled, story-worthy experiences.

### Ambient richness / texture
Ordinary delight, noticing, participation, sensory richness, rituals, small connection, everyday beauty.

A rich life needs both.

## Heuristic dimensions
Potential experience value may consider:
- personal fit
- excitement/aliveness
- awe
- novelty
- immersion
- connection
- participation
- beauty
- story
- serendipity
- rarity
- learning/mastery
- contribution
minus:
- money cost
- friction
- attention demand
- exhaustion
- risk
- crowding
- opportunity cost

Do not pretend this is objective science. Return reason codes and confidence.

## Budgets
- money
- time
- energy
- friction
- attention
- unstructured time

Attention protection is a first-class objective:
> Eyes up.

## Experience Lens Engine
Sometimes change the lens instead of the place.
Examples:
- photograph evidence of repair
- find five signs of previous generations
- listen for the farthest sound
- follow one color
- notice thresholds/doorways
- photograph silence

This enables richness without requiring travel, spending, or events.
