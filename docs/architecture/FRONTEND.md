# Frontend — Life Board

## Design character
Calm, warm, alive, spacious. Not a productivity dashboard and not an RPG HUD.

## Home
Headline:
> What kind of day is available?

Primary opportunity cards:
1. RIGHT HERE
2. GO SOMEWHERE
3. YOUR EDGE
4. SURPRISE ME
5. REST

Each card should answer:
- what
- why now
- time/cost/energy
- difficulty
- what to bring/do next
- “why this?” explanation
- optional surprise protection

## What's Alive
Visualize thread states:
- Burning
- Glowing
- Sparking
- Embers
- Completed

Avoid fake precision percentages unless based on an explicit scoring model and useful.

## Current Edges
Maximum three.
Show domain, current edge, challenge setting, recent evidence.

## Journeys
Interest/hobby journeys. Focus on next lived step, not course completion.

## Atlas
Moments, firsts & crossings, places, people, stories, creations, awe, lessons.
User chooses what becomes a Moment.

## Pulse
Future openings:
- seasonal phenomena
- events
- weather windows
- travel anomalies
- people/commitments
- unfinished excitement threads

## Travel surface
Trip Pulse:
- realistic total cost
- route options
- accommodation
- timing
- memory potential
- fit
- friction/attention burden
- Recon need-to-know
- Mythos/story layer
- Adventure Window
- verification status

## Interaction
Default to progressive disclosure.
The app should be able to say very little beautifully.
