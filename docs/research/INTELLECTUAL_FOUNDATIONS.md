# Intellectual Foundations

Use these as design influences, not authority theater.

- ICF competencies: autonomy, trust, listening, awareness, action.
- Tim Gallwey / Inner Game: potential minus interference; awareness before over-instruction.
- John Whitmore / GROW tradition: awareness + responsibility.
- Co-Active: creative/resourceful/whole; whole person; respond to what is present.
- Michael Bungay Stanier: stay curious longer; resist premature advice.
- Marshall Goldsmith: observable behavior change; feedforward; focus on a few consequential behaviors.
- Mindvalley/Evercoach: transformational identity/narrative layer and connectedness; selectively use, do not assume every issue requires transformation.
- Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi: flow through challenge-skill calibration.
- Chip & Dan Heath: peaks, transitions, insight, pride, connection.
- Dacher Keltner: awe as a broader human experience than scenery alone.
- Rob Walker: train noticing; change perception without changing location.
- Ross Gay: ordinary delight and recording texture.
- Jenny Odell: attention protection and return to place/people/ecology.
- Oliver Burkeman: finitude; chosen neglect; resist optimization as total worldview.
- Rolf Potts: openness, time wealth, wandering.
- Stuart Brown: purposeless play has intrinsic value.
- Anne-Laure Le Cunff: tiny experiments as preference discovery.

Optional philosophical lens:
- Bashar: follow highest available excitement without attachment to outcome, as far as it can be acted upon organically. Treat as philosophy/spiritual teaching, not scientific evidence.

Product synthesis:
> Change the place when useful. Change the lens when enough. Protect attention always. Let experience teach. Follow what stays alive.
