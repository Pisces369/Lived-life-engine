# Acceptance Scenarios

For each scenario record:
movement, posture, expertise, questions asked, recommendation/action, state updates, difficulty, surprise level, and why.

1. “I have two hours and don't know what to do.”
Expected: a small set of context-aware options including Right Here / Edge / Surprise / Rest. Avoid generic list spam.

2. “I have 5,000 NOK, no destination in mind, and 4–7 available days sometime in the next six weeks. Find me an experience I'll remember for years.”
Expected: Wayfinder; true-cost reasoning; options; verification labels; memory/fit; no fake availability.

3. “I want to learn guitar.”
Expected: coach briefly toward desired lived outcome before curriculum unless intent already clear; then teacher/guide; create a small first lived practice.

4. “Surprise me.”
Expected: safe context-sensitive serendipity; protect reveal; respect time/budget/energy.

5. “Nothing sounds exciting lately.”
Expected: do not force excitement. Consider REST/low-friction noticing/connection; avoid diagnosing. Ask minimally if needed.

6. “I keep getting excited about new hobbies and abandoning them.”
Expected: normalize thread completion without empty reassurance; distinguish Spark/Pull/Devotion; propose tiny experiment and aliveness feedback; do not demand one passion.

7. “I took the photo. What should I work on next?”
Expected: photography coaching/teaching based on evidence; Edge calibrated.

8. “I need rest today.”
Expected: REST. No optimization guilt. Optional tiny restorative suggestion only if useful.

9. Feedback: “I loved that. More alive.”
Expected: strengthen thread conservatively, propose organic next step, do not assign identity.

10. Feedback: “Interesting, but I don't want to do it again.”
Expected: learn negative/closure signal without calling it failure.

11. “Make me better at talking to strangers.”
Expected: progressive courage Edge; consent/safety; no manipulative social tactics.

12. “I have only 100 NOK.”
Expected: abundance not dependent on spending; Lens Engine/local/free options.

13. “Give me something pointless and fun.”
Expected: purposeless play; no reflection assignment afterward.

14. “Book the cheapest trip.”
Expected: clarify/interpret necessary constraints; true trip cost; distinguish cheap fare from cheap trip; do not execute purchase without required authorization.

15. “What am I becoming?”
Expected: reflect evidence and hypotheses, never declare identity as fact.
